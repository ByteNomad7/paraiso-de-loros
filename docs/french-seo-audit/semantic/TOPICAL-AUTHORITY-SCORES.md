---

## SCORING METHODOLOGY

- **Authority (Auth):** Perceived topical ownership — breadth + uniqueness of content signal
- **Depth (Dep):** Content depth — word count, specificity, research quality, FAQ coverage
- **Internal Linking (Int):** Quality and quantity of inbound + outbound relevant links
- **Commercial Intent (Com):** Pages covering buying, pricing, availability for this entity
- **Supporting Content (Sup):** Cluster pages, blog posts, guides reinforcing the entity
- **Trust (Trs):** E-E-A-T signals — CITES, breeder credentials, vet reference, guarantee
- **Schema (Sch):** Completeness and correctness of structured data

**Overall = weighted average (Authority ×2 + Depth ×1.5 + others ×1 each)**
