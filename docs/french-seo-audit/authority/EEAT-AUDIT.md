## WHAT E-E-A-T MEANS FOR THIS SITE

This is a YMYL-adjacent site (Your Money or Your Life). Parrot ownership involves:
- Financial commitment (hundreds to thousands of euros)
- Living animal welfare
- Legal compliance (CITES, animal registration)
- Health risks (psittacosis)

Google applies elevated E-E-A-T scrutiny to YMYL-adjacent content. The bar is higher than for a hobby blog.

## EXPERIENCE (E)

*"Does the content creator have first-hand or life experience with the topic?"*

### Current signals ✅
- 25 years of active parrot breeding — stated across multiple pages
- `/fr/nos-installations/` — physical facility evidence (photos, description)
- `/fr/galerie/` — 12 gallery categories showing real birds, real facility
- `/fr/elevage-socialisation/` gallery — evidence of hand-raising process
- Species-level content that reflects genuine husbandry knowledge (accurate lifespan data, correct CITES classifications)
- `/fr/perroquets-eleves-a-la-main/` — first-hand methodology description

### Weaknesses ❌
- **No named individual** — "Paraíso de Aves" is a brand, not a person. Google's Quality Guidelines specifically value content attributable to a named, credentialed individual.
- **No author bylines** — none of the 157 pages attribute content to a specific person
- **No dated expertise milestones** — "25 years" is mentioned but not anchored to verifiable facts (founding year, first CITES permit year, etc.)
- **No testimonials or case studies** — no first-person buyer experiences documented on the site
- **Gallery images lack EXIF metadata descriptions** — missed opportunity to signal real-world experience

### Recommendations
1. **Create a named breeder page** — e.g., "Pedro García — Éleveur depuis 2001" — with a photo, biography, founding story, key milestones. Even a pseudonym is less effective than a real name.
2. **Add author attribution to all cluster pages and blog posts** — even "Par l'équipe Paraíso de Aves, éleveurs depuis 2001"
3. **Add 3–5 buyer case studies** to `/fr/eleveur-perroquets/` — real adoption stories (anonymised if needed), showing the experience of buying through the site
4. **Date-stamp content** — all pages should have a "Mis à jour le [date]" signal showing active maintenance
5. **Video content** — a 60-second facility tour video embedded on `/fr/nos-installations/` would be among the strongest experience signals possible

## EXPERTISE (E)

*"Does the content demonstrate genuine knowledge of the subject?"*

### Current signals ✅
- Correct scientific names (Psittacus erithacus, Anodorhynchus hyacinthinus, etc.) on species pages
- Accurate CITES classification (Annexe I for Gris du Gabon and Ara Hyacinthe, II for others)
- Pepperberg/Alex research referenced in talking cluster — cites scientific source
- Accurate lifespan data (50–70 years for Gris, cross-referenced with known ranges)
- Hand-raising methodology described with real nutritional and behavioural detail
- FAQPage answers on species pages show genuine depth (moulting, hormonal cycles, independent play)

### Weaknesses ❌
- **Health content is thin** — `/fr/connaissances/sante/` is a single page. A genuine expert on parrot ownership should be able to discuss psittacosis, aspergillosis, and common health issues in depth.
- **Nutrition content is shallow** — one blog post and one product page for a topic with enormous depth. Real breeders know the difference between a Cacatoès and a Gris du Gabon's dietary needs precisely.
- **No cited external sources beyond Pepperberg** — citing ornithological studies, CITES official databases, or French veterinary guidance would significantly strengthen expertise signals.
- **Training content is limited to language** — a genuine parrot expert covers clicker training, recall, behavioural modification. The current gap signals incomplete expertise to Google.
- **No comparison of breeding approaches** — a genuine expert discusses parent-raising vs hand-raising tradeoffs, which is entirely absent.

### Recommendations
1. **Cite sources throughout** — link to CITES official site (cites.org), DREAL (French environmental authority), relevant ornithological literature. External authoritative links signal expertise.
2. **Build the health and nutrition clusters** (see SUPPORTING-CLUSTERS.md) — this is the single largest expertise gap.
3. **Add "Les conseils de l'éleveur" sections** to Tier 1 species pages — a 200-word first-person recommendation section per species.
4. **Reference veterinary guidance** on health pages — "consulter un vétérinaire NAC agréé" with a link to the SNVEL (French veterinary association) or similar.
5. **Add a methodology page** for hand-raising — how the breeder does it, what formula they use, at what age, what they watch for. This is irreplaceable first-hand expertise.

## TRUSTWORTHINESS (T)

*"Is this website safe, honest, and transparent?"*

### Current signals ✅
- Legal compliance prominently featured (CITES documentation, health guarantee)
- No fabricated prices — all price pages use "prix sur demande" correctly
- No fabricated reviews or ratings
- Contact email published (paraisodeloros@gmail.com)
- Delivery policy with honest timeframe (2–4 jours)
- Health guarantee with clear terms
- Honest "à venir" placeholders on pillar page (now replaced with live links)
- No clickbait titles or deceptive claims

### Weaknesses ❌
- **Gmail address** — paraisodeloros@gmail.com signals a one-person operation, not an established business. A professional @paraisodeaves.com email address is a material trust signal.
- **No physical address on contact page** — GDPR compliance + trust requires a verifiable business address
- **No privacy policy or terms** — legal pages (politique de confidentialité, conditions générales de vente) are absent or hidden
- **No secure payment page reference** — buyers have no information about how payment is processed securely
- **No FAQ covering scam prevention** — the market for exotic birds has scam problems; a dedicated anti-scam page would build trust and rank for "arnaque perroquet" queries
- **No return/refund policy** — beyond the health guarantee, there's no documented process for dispute resolution
- **Cookie consent** — a professional GDPR-compliant cookie notice is either missing or minimal
- **Organization schema on homepage uses a Spanish address** — the company is based in Llíria, Spain, but serves France. This address mismatch in LocalBusiness schema may confuse Google's local entity resolution.

### Recommendations
1. **Professional email domain** — migrate to paraisodeloros@paraisodeaves.com (or similar). This is one of the lowest-cost, highest-trust improvements available.
2. **Add legal pages** — /fr/mentions-legales/, /fr/politique-de-confidentialite/, /fr/conditions-vente/. Required by French law for commercial websites.
3. **Add a physical contact address** — even a PO box or Spanish head office address. Buyers need a verifiable entity.
4. **Build /fr/arnaques-vente-perroquet/** — a trust page warning buyers about common scams, positioning the site as the safe alternative.
5. **Add secure payment information** — even if payment is done by bank transfer, describing the secure process builds confidence.
6. **Separate FR LocalBusiness schema from ES address** — use `areaServed: France` but clarify that delivery is from Spain. Transparency over confusion.
