## TOPICS WHERE SPANISH IS STRONGER

1. **Blog volume** — 188 ES vs 11 FR. ES dominates entirely.
2. **Individual FAQ pages** — 37 ES vs 0 FR individual pages.
3. **Buyer-type commercial pages** — /loros-que-hablan/, /loros-para-familia/, /loros-apartamento/, /loros-bebe/ all missing in FR.
4. **Knowledge sub-hubs** — ES has 13 connaissances pages including adiestramiento, instalaciones, cria, viajes — FR missing these.
5. **Blog clusters** — ES has dedicated behaviour, training, health, housing clusters. FR has none.

## TOPICS WHERE FRENCH CAN SURPASS SPANISH

1. **Species guides (/fr/especies/)** — FR already has 21 species vs ES's 16. Adding depth to each would make FR the site's strongest species hub.
2. **France-specific CITES & legal content** — FR covers French CITES law, not Spanish. Unique content Google can only credit to FR section.
3. **French city coverage** — 50 city pages with LocalBusiness schema is competitive; adding a cities hub page and regional pages would put FR ahead of ES.
4. **FAQ cluster** — If FR builds 37+ individual FAQ pages targeting French-language queries, it would immediately surpass ES in FAQ coverage since many French queries are not direct translations (e.g. French-specific food/law queries differ from Spanish ones).
5. **Ara Chloroptère** — No ES species page exists; FR has the only page on the site. With a commercial page added, this species could rank exclusively for FR.
6. **Grand Alexandre / Perruche Royale** — No ES equivalents. FR is already uniquely positioned.
