## E-E-A-T FOR EXOTIC ANIMAL BREEDERS — THE STAKES

This site sells living animals costing €500–€20,000+. Google's Quality Raters classify it as YMYL-adjacent (Your Money or Your Life) — the same scrutiny applied to medical, financial, and legal content. The bar is:

**"Would a reasonable person trust this source with a significant financial and emotional decision involving a living being?"**

Currently: the answer is "possibly, if they dig far enough." The goal is: "yes, immediately."

**EE-EX02 — Case observations in health/behaviour content**  
Health and behaviour articles gain enormous E-E-A-T value from "In our 25 years, we have seen..." observations. These cannot be fabricated — they must be genuine breeder observations.

**EE-EX03 — Process photography timeline**  
A "Birth to delivery" photo timeline on /fr/perroquets-eleves-a-la-main/ showing:
- Week 1: hatchling (if available and ethical to show)
- Week 4: first feathering
- Week 8: first solid foods
- Week 12: first human interaction
- Week 16+: weaning complete, ready for adoption

This is the most powerful experience signal possible — it demonstrates direct involvement that no competitor can fake.

**EE-EX04 — Video content**  
One short video (60–90 seconds) of the breeder with birds — either a brief introduction or a "day in the life" of the facility — would single-handedly elevate experience signals above every text-based competitor. Embedded on homepage + /fr/eleveur-perroquets/ + /fr/nos-installations/.

## EXPERTISE (E)

*Deep knowledge of the subject demonstrated through content quality*

### Current expertise signals
- Scientific names used correctly across species pages ✅
- CITES classifications accurate ✅
- Lifespan and behavioural data accurate ✅
- Talking cluster shows genuine knowledge of psittacine cognition ✅
- Pepperberg research cited ✅

### Missing expertise signals

**EE-EP01 — Species-specific veterinary knowledge**  
Health content currently has ~6% coverage. A genuine 25-year breeder knows the common health issues of each species better than any general vet. Content like "Les Gris du Gabon sont prédisposés à la carence en calcium — voici ce que nous faisons pour prévenir cela dans notre élevage" is irreplaceable expertise content.

**EE-EP02 — Nutrition expertise by species**  
Same pattern — species-specific dietary knowledge from observed experience. "Nous avons observé que les Aras Hyacinthe consomment principalement des noix de palme dans la nature — voici comment nous reproduisons cela en captivité."

**EE-EP03 — Training expertise beyond talking**  
The talking cluster demonstrates language-training expertise. A similarly deep cluster on recall training, taming, and positive reinforcement would demonstrate full-spectrum expertise.

**EE-EP04 — Cite external authoritative sources**  
Currently the site cites Pepperberg on one page. Every article should reference 1–2 authoritative external sources:
- CITES official database (checklist.cites.org) for species status
- World Parrot Trust (parrots.org) for conservation data
- French veterinary authority (veterinaire.fr) for health guidelines
- Scientific papers where available (Google Scholar provides free access to many ornithological papers)

**This does not weaken the site** — citing sources is what authoritative sites do. It signals that the content has been verified against the best available knowledge.

**EE-EP05 — Content depth standards**  
Set a minimum standard for all expert content:
- Species guides: 2,500 words minimum
- Health/nutrition articles: 1,500 words minimum with cited sources
- Training guides: 2,000 words minimum
- Price guides: 1,800 words minimum with honest pricing context

## TRUSTWORTHINESS (T)

*Honesty, transparency, safety of the website and content*

### Current trust signals
- CITES documentation transparency ✅
- Honest "prix sur demande" pricing ✅
- 72-hour health guarantee ✅
- No fabricated reviews ✅

### Missing trust signals (see also TRUST-SIGNALS.md for full detail)

**EE-TR01 — Legal pages (IMMEDIATE — legal requirement)**  
Mentions Légales, CGV, Politique de Confidentialité.  
These are not optional in France. Their absence signals either ignorance of French law or deliberate non-compliance. Neither builds trust.

**EE-TR02 — Professional email domain**  
paraisodeloros@gmail.com → info@paraisodeaves.com  
One of the lowest-effort, highest-trust improvements available.

**EE-TR03 — GDPR-compliant cookie consent**  
GA4 is running. Without a compliant consent mechanism, the site is in breach of GDPR. Irrespective of the legal risk, a Quality Rater seeing a site without cookie consent on a French commercial website will flag it.

**EE-TR04 — Transparent "not included" section**  
The health guarantee page already lists what is NOT covered — this is a trust signal (not trying to oversell). Expand this pattern to the processus-adoption page: be explicit about what happens if a buyer changes their mind, if the bird is injured in transit, etc. Radical transparency builds more trust than over-promising.

**EE-TR05 — Ownership disclosure**  
The site is a Spanish breeding facility selling to France. This should be stated transparently rather than implied: "Notre élevage est situé à Llíria, Valence (Espagne). Nous livrons dans toute la France." Some buyers may feel misled if they later discover the breeder is in Spain.
