# Trust Signals — Complete Audit & Recommendations

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Scope:** Every trust-building component across the French section  
**Policy:** No fabricated credentials. Every signal recommended is either already factual or provably buildable.

---

## TRUST SIGNAL FRAMEWORK

Trust signals for a premium exotic animal breeder operate on four levels:

1. **Institutional** — legal, regulatory, professional body
2. **Expert** — credentials, methodology, experience
3. **Social** — testimonials, case studies, community
4. **Process** — transparency about what happens before/during/after purchase

The site has reasonable Level 2 coverage and almost no Level 1, 3, or 4 coverage.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 1: HEALTH & VETERINARY CARE

### Current state
- /fr/garantie-sante/ exists — describes 72-hour health guarantee
- "Bilan vétérinaire complet" mentioned across pages
- "Vétérinaire aviaire" referenced on garantie page
- No vet clinic named, no vet certificate shown, no photo of vet check

### What should exist

**TS-H01 — Expanded Health Guarantee Page**  
The current page is ~400 words covering: what's included, 72-hour window, what's excluded.  
A premium health guarantee page should cover:
- Pre-departure veterinary examination — what tests are run
- Parasite screening — internal and external
- Weight check and nutritional assessment
- Plumage and feather condition assessment
- Beak and claw inspection
- Full documentation provided: species certificate, health certificate
- What to do within 72h if concerned — step by step
- What constitutes a claimable condition (clear list)
- What is excluded and why (honest transparency)
- Photographs of the examination process (real facility photos)

**TS-H02 — Veterinary Care Statement**  
A named avian veterinary clinic, or at minimum "notre vétérinaire aviaire agréé en Espagne/France" with the accreditation type. This does not require naming a specific vet if there are privacy concerns — "vétérinaire NAC agréé" is sufficient if verifiable.

**TS-H03 — DNA Testing Section**  
Currently referenced but unexplained. Should have:
- What DNA testing confirms (sex, no genetic disease markers)
- When it is done in the bird's development
- What documentation the buyer receives
- Why this matters (prevents buyer's regret, health planning)

**Recommended page:** /fr/test-adn-perroquet/ or section on /fr/garantie-sante/

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 2: CITES COMPLIANCE

### Current state
- CITES documented across multiple pages
- "Documentation CITES incluse" on garantie/eleveur pages
- /fr/connaissances/cites/ exists (thin)
- CITES badge in hero/sidebar areas

### What should exist

**TS-C01 — CITES Transparency Block**  
Every species page and commercial page should carry a consistent "Documentation incluse" block:
```
✓ Certificat CITES (si espèce Annexe I/II)
✓ Bague d'identification officielle
✓ Certificat vétérinaire de bonne santé
✓ Carnet d'alimentation et de soins
```

**TS-C02 — Noyau Zoologique Badge**  
"Noyau Zoologique Officiel" is already stated in the eleveur page. This should appear as a badge in the footer of all 157 pages, not just the breeder page.

**TS-C03 — CITES Registration Number**  
If the breeder has a CITES registration number (which they should if operating as a Noyau Zoologique), displaying this number is one of the strongest possible trust signals. It is independently verifiable.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 3: BREEDING & HUSBANDRY

### Current state
- /fr/perroquets-eleves-a-la-main/ — methodology page exists
- /fr/nos-installations/ — facility page exists
- No video content
- No named breeder
- No photos of the hand-raising process

### What should exist

**TS-B01 — Breeder Biography Section**  
A named breeder with photo and biography on /fr/eleveur-perroquets/. This is the single most impactful trust element missing from the site. Premium animal breeders worldwide use personal stories ("I started with my first Grey in 1999...") because they work — they make buyers feel safe.

**TS-B02 — Hand-Raising Process Visual**  
A gallery or visual walkthrough of hand-raising: feeding schedule, weaning stages, socialisation milestones. Parents trust a breeder more when they can see the process their new companion went through.

**TS-B03 — Breeding Philosophy Statement**  
A concise statement on the /fr/eleveur-perroquets/ page covering:
- Why hand-raising vs parent-raising
- What the birds eat during development
- How they are socialised with humans before leaving
- The commitment to never shipping a stressed or unhealthy bird

**TS-B04 — Parrot Welfare Statement**  
A dedicated section on welfare — enrichment provided, quality of life at the facility, CITES conservation alignment. This converts price-sensitive buyers who are on the fence: they see that paying more means the bird had a better start.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 4: DELIVERY & TRANSPORT

### Current state
- /fr/livraison/ — delivery page with timelines (Paris 2–3 days, France 2–5 days)
- /fr/caisses-de-transport/ — IATA crate page exists
- No carrier named, no tracking described, no photo of crate setup

### What should exist

**TS-D01 — Transport Standards Section**  
Expand /fr/livraison/ to include:
- Named transport company type (transporteur spécialisé animaux vivants)
- IATA crate homologation number or standard
- Temperature control during transport
- Maximum transit time guarantee
- What happens if transit is delayed
- Photo of the bird's transport setup (crate, ventilation, food/water provision)

**TS-D02 — Delivery Timeline Visualisation**  
A simple visual flow: Day 1 (Confirmation + paperwork) → Day 2 (Vet check + crate prep) → Day 2-5 (Transit) → Delivery + first day guidance. This makes the abstract process concrete and reduces buyer anxiety.

**TS-D03 — Accueil du Perroquet Guide**  
A dedicated page (or prominent section) on what to do when the bird arrives:
- Setting up the space in advance
- First 24 hours — what is normal (stress behaviour)
- First week feeding and interaction
- When to be concerned vs when to be patient

This builds post-sale trust and reduces "panic contact" situations.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 5: COMMERCIAL PROCESS

### Current state
- /fr/processus-adoption/ — 6-step process: Contact → Vérification → Acompte → Préparation → Livraison → Suivi
- "Lifetime support" stated
- No payment method details
- No acompte (deposit) amount stated

### What should exist

**TS-P01 — Payment Security Statement**  
Even if payment is by bank transfer, a brief explanation of: what the deposit covers, when the balance is due, what happens if a bird becomes ill before delivery, and how refunds are handled. Buyers at this price point need this.

**TS-P02 — Deposit & Balance Policy**  
"Un acompte de [X]% est demandé pour confirmer votre réservation. Le solde est dû à la livraison." This single sentence reduces purchase anxiety dramatically.

**TS-P03 — After-Sales Support Process**  
"Lifetime support" is stated in the adoption process but not explained. What does this mean in practice?
- Email support: yes, paraisodeloros@gmail.com (should be @paraisodeaves.com)
- Response time for post-adoption queries: 24h?
- Dietary questions, behaviour questions — who answers?
- Emergency vet guidance?

A concrete description of what "lifetime support" looks like converts the abstract promise into a real differentiator.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 6: SOCIAL PROOF

### Current state
- Zero testimonials
- Zero reviews
- Zero named customer references
- Zero "X buyers satisfied" counters

### What should exist

**TS-S01 — Buyer Testimonials (minimum 3)**  
Anonymised testimonials ("Marie L., Paris — Gris du Gabon, adopté juin 2023") are acceptable and fully honest. These should appear on:
- Homepage (in the existing trust section)
- /fr/perroquets-disponibles/
- /fr/eleveur-perroquets/
- /fr/acheter-perroquet/

Format: Name (first name + initial), city, species, adoption year, 2–3 sentence quote.

**TS-S02 — Counter Statistics**  
"Plus de X familles satisfaites depuis 1999" — if verifiable, this is powerful. Even a conservative estimate ("plusieurs centaines de familles") is useful.

**TS-S03 — Gallery of Previous Buyers' Birds**  
With explicit permission: a gallery section of birds in their homes, sent by buyers. This is standard on premium breeder sites internationally and is the most authentic social proof possible.

## SIGNAL CATEGORY 7: LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

### Current state
- No Mentions Légales page
- No CGV (Conditions Générales de Vente)
- No Politique de Confidentialité / RGPD
- Gmail address
- No physical address on contact page

### What must exist (legal requirement in France)

**TS-L01 — Mentions Légales page** (REQUIRED)  
Must contain: company legal name, address, SIRET or equivalent, VAT if applicable, hosting company details, editor contact.

**TS-L02 — CGV (Conditions Générales de Vente)** (REQUIRED)  
Must contain: payment terms, delivery terms, right of withdrawal (or its limitation for living animals), health guarantee terms, dispute resolution.

**TS-L03 — Politique de Confidentialité** (REQUIRED — GDPR)  
Must contain: what data is collected, how it is used, how long it is retained, rights of data subjects.

**TS-L04 — Professional email domain**  
Replace paraisodeloros@gmail.com with info@paraisodeaves.com or similar throughout all 157 pages and the contact form.

**TS-L05 — Cookie consent**  
If GA4 is running (which it is: G-4007YHH4H9), a GDPR-compliant cookie consent banner is legally required.
