# Premium Brand Roadmap — Paraíso de Aves FR

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Goal:** Transform the French section into the most trustworthy and premium parrot website in the French-speaking market  
**Ranked by:** ROI · SEO impact · Conversion impact · Implementation complexity

---

## VISION

The finished state of this roadmap produces a website where any French buyer — from a first-time enquirer to a seasoned bird keeper — immediately perceives:

> "This is the most serious, trustworthy, and knowledgeable parrot breeder I can find in France."

Not because it is the biggest or the most expensive. Because every element of the experience signals expertise, transparency, and care.

---

## THE THREE GAPS TO CLOSE

**Gap 1 — Compliance** (legal, GDPR): Closes within 2 weeks. High risk if ignored.  
**Gap 2 — Trust architecture** (professional identity, guarantees, transparency): Closes within 4 weeks. Immediate conversion uplift.  
**Gap 3 — Content authority** (behaviour, health, nutrition clusters): Closes within 3–6 months. Long-term organic growth engine.
